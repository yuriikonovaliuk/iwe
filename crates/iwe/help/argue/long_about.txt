Compute the dialectical standing of the graph's claims: which are in, out or
undecided given the objections raised against them and the premises they rest
on. A reading, never a gate — the command always exits 0.

Nodes are the claim types (fact, pattern, model, stance, conjecture,
hypothesis) and objections. An objection attacks the target of its `Against`
section — or the premise in its `Undermines` section when its kind is
`undermines`; an answered objection attacks nothing; a conceded one stands.
`Rests on` links between nodes are support. Standing is the grounded
extension with deductive support: in when every attacker is out and every
premise is in; out when some attacker is in or some premise is out; otherwise
undecided. See `iwe docs argue`.
